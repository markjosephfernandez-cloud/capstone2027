Data  augmentation  
The  following  data  augmentation  techniques  are  applied  before  model  training  to  improve  
model

●  Horizontal  flipping  
●  Vertical  flipping  
●  Brightness  adjustment  
●  Contrast  adjustment  
●  Random  cropping  
●  Image  resizing

●  70%  of  the  dataset  is  used  for  training  the  AI  models.  
●  15%  is  used  for  validation  during  training  to  monitor  performance  and  adjust  model

●  15%  is  reserved  for  final  testing  using  images  that  were  not  included  during  training.
